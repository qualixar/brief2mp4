# Brief 02: Three.js, "ORBIT"

Make a 10-second 3D shot in Three.js.

- Delivery: final.mp4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 10.0 s, H.264 + AAC.
- Palette: night sky #14112B, planet coral #FF7A59, moons gold #FFC857, text cream #FFF4E8.
- A low-poly planet (flat-shaded) with a thin tilted ring, in the centre of the frame.
- Three small moons orbit the planet at three different speeds and distances. Each one passes in front of the planet at least once.
- About 1,500 stars, placed with a seeded random generator, so every render is identical.
- Camera: starts wide and slow-pushes in over the whole shot, with a gentle orbit. One continuous move, no cuts.
- At 6.5 s the word "ORBIT" rises in from below in HTML text, 140 px, wide letter spacing, below the planet. It holds until the end.
- Nothing in the frame is cut off at any time, and the planet stays lit on the side facing the camera.
- Sound: a quiet soundtrack with a low accent at 6.5 s.
