# Brief 01: kinetic type, "Finish something today"

Make a 12-second kinetic typography video.

- Delivery: final.mp4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 12.0 s, H.264 + AAC.
- Background: warm paper #F2EDE4. Text: ink #16130F. Accent: signal orange #FF5A1F. Typeface: Space Grotesk (Google Fonts), bold.
- Three lines, in this order, each one replacing the last:
  1. 0.3-3.5 s: "Ideas are cheap."
  2. 3.5-7.0 s: "Finished is rare."
  3. 7.0-12.0 s: "Finish something today."
- Every word arrives with a masked rise, one word at a time.
- An orange bar wipes across the frame between lines (at 3.5 s and 7.0 s). After the last wipe it shrinks into an underline under the word "today."
- The last line is the hero: it lands by 8.0 s and holds still and fully readable until 12.0 s.
- Sound: a quiet soundtrack with a low accent on each wipe (3.5 s and 7.0 s).
- Text never touches the frame edge. Headlines at least 120 px tall.
