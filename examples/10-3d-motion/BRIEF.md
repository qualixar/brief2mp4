# Brief 10: five motions, one studio

Make a 24-second film that shows five kinds of Blender motion in one place, each act rendered headless from a recipe, cut to music.

- Delivery: final.mp4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 24.0 s, H.264 + AAC, music at -14 LUFS.
- One world: a pale seamless studio with no horizon, soft shadows, the same palette in every act. Vermilion for the hero object, cobalt for the second colour, charcoal for type. No teal, no mint.
- Act 1, caption "Cloth drop": a cloth sheet falls onto a sphere and drapes over it, settling into folds.
- Act 2, caption "Hinged chain": a chain of seven links hangs from a post and is released from the side; the whole swing stays in frame.
- Act 3, caption "Folding": a sheet folds in half three times; its underside is a second colour, so every fold shows it. The camera follows the stack as it shrinks.
- Act 4, caption "Walking, feet planted": a mannequin walks forward with an upright, natural posture, the camera following. No foot slides.
- Act 5, caption "Seamless loop": a gem turns once per bar of the music, bobs, and carries a rocking ring. It loops with no visible join.
- Camera: slow, steady moves that all drift the same way, so the cuts feel like one camera; the last act holds still.
- Captions: small lower thirds, bottom left, fading in after each cut and out before the next.
- Between acts: short dissolves on the bar lines of the music, no dips and no pops.
- Sound: openly licensed music (CC0 or CC BY), with the attribution in the example folder.
