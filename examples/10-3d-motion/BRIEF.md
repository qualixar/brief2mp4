# Brief 10: five motions, one film

Make a 20-second film that shows five kinds of Blender motion, one per act of 4 seconds, each rendered headless from a recipe.

- Delivery: final.mp4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 20.0 s, H.264 + AAC, quiet soundtrack.
- Act 1, 0-4 s, caption "Cloth drop": a cloth sheet falls onto a sphere and drapes over it.
- Act 2, 4-8 s, caption "Hinged chain": a chain of seven links is released from the side and swings in one plane.
- Act 3, 8-12 s, caption "Folding": a sheet folds in half three times, ending as a thick strip.
- Act 4, 12-16 s, caption "Walking, feet planted": a figure walks forward across a ruled floor, the camera following. No foot slides.
- Act 5, 16-20 s, caption "Seamless loop": a gem turns once, bobs, and carries a rocking ring. The loop plays twice with no visible join.
- Look: a dark slate stage, amber subjects, brick-red accents, cream captions. No teal, no mint. Soft shadows. One camera that holds still, except the follower in act 4.
- Each caption sits low and to the left, inside the safe margin, for its whole act.
- Between acts the picture dips through the stage colour for a fifth of a second.
- Sound: a quiet bed, with a low accent where the cloth lands (about 1.4 s) and a click at each act start.
