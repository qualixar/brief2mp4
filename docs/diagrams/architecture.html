How brief2mp4 works

One loop an agent runs in Claude Code or Codex: every line of your brief becomes a check, and nothing ships until the checks pass.
1
Your brief
Length, size, palette, the exact words, what happens when, and a "done when" list.
BRIEF.md
2
Spec
Delivery table, exact text, look, a timed beat sheet, one checkable line per requirement.

3

Formats guide picks the beat sheet; each beat goes to the tool that fits it.

optional3D packCAD parts, materials, physics, explode

Compose

Finish

finish.sh
6
Self-check
Measure the file: PASS, WARN or FAIL.
Look at stills at every beat. Mark each brief line met or not, with evidence.
fix the worst miss, render again (max 3 rounds)
